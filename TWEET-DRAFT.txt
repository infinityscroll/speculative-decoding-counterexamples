An ICLR 2025 speculative-decoding formula predicts 100% optimal acceptance where the true optimum is 79.96%. A positive-probability family widens the error toward 100 percentage points. Exact proof + reproducible checks; a correction to the formula, not a measured speedup.

Follow-up:
The source uses successive draws without replacement but evaluates probabilities for a batch conditioned on distinct draws. We give explicit optimal couplings and matching bounds. Separately written Codex-assisted checks agree. Priority and external review remain unverified.
